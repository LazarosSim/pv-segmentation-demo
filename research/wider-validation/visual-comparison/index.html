How to interpret these comparisons

The cyan outlines reproduce the current published demo exactly. The orange outlines are the saved, independently scored table-line experiment. None of these outlines is redrawn or snapped for presentation. The full image views retain partial panels at the image edges.

The author’s white reference outlines are approximate dataset annotations, not verified measurements of every metal-frame edge. They were used for evaluation, not supplied to the border-fitting experiment. A closer reference match can still leave a visible physical border error.

Pink marks, when enabled, show intersections whose area exceeds 1% of the smaller panel outline. Thin lines can still appear to touch because of their display width; the highlighted regions use actual enclosed shape areas.

On these three images, matches at a strict 90% shared-area requirement rose from 323 to 339 out of 483 references. Substantial overlap pairs rose from 12 to 15, with 11 newly introduced pairs. On the separate twenty-image test, strict matches stayed at 587 out of 845 references. These showcase views do not establish performance on new sites.

Source: Czech Technical University in Prague photovoltaic module dataset. Download these display geometries and provenance.
