Wider spacing

The old table lattice leaves about 1.4 native pixels between outlines where two visible frame bands are about 14 pixels apart. V6 already retains that wider gap. The joint separator starts from V6 and preserves positive gaps; it does not invent panels to fill a grid.

Overlapping outlines

The two reported V6 crossings cover about 5.3% and 8.4% of the smaller panel polygons. The old guard allowed inherited crossings. The new separator jointly moves facing edges where a unique source band passes its fixed checks. Both reported pairs fail those checks and remain unchanged. Flagging an overlap is not repairing it.
